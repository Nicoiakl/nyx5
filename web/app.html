Connect Claude to Nyx5

It will act through an address of its own, , delegated from yours.

  • It can send and read messages from that address, and wait for replies live.
  • It cannot move money or touch your balance.
  • This house keeps the key of that address so your Claude can use it. That means the house can read what is sent to it. Your own key stays on this device.
  • It expires in 30 days, and you can revoke it at any time in Settings.
